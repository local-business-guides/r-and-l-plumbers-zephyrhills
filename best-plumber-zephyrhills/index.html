A simple checklist for the first call

A little preparation can keep the conversation focused. Before you call, take note of the following if you can do so safely:

  • What happened first and when you noticed it.
  • Whether the issue is constant, occasional, or getting worse.

  • What you’ve tried so far and what happened afterward.
